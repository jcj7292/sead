[Jinyang Li](https://seadlab.com/members/jinyang-li.html) and [Ranhee Yoon](https://seadlab.com/members/ranhee-yoon.html) presented our latest research in the poster session at [VT Manufacturing Day](https://me.vt.edu/research/mfgday.html) held at Virginia Tech. The presentations are listed below.

Title: **[High-Dimensional Design of Kirigami Patches Metamaterials Using Physics-based Machine Learning with Uncertainty Quantification](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aei.2026.104776)**<br>
Authors: **Jinyang Li, Jyotshna Bali, Suyi Li & Jie Chen**, Department of Mechanical Engineering, Virginia Tech

Jinyang Li presented his work on Manufacturing day poster session about Kirigami structure design. The research establishes a data-efficient framework for designing kirigami metamaterials by integrating physics-based machine learning, uncertainty quantification, and Bayesian optimization. The proposed Phy-SNGP accurately predicts strain-dependent Poisson’s ratios and is validated through simulation and experiment of near-zero Poisson’s ratio structures and conformable knee patches.

Title: **Autonomous Mobile Robot for Power Line Conductor Health Inspection: Machine Learning for Damage Detection**<br>
Authors: **Ranhee Yoon & Jie Chen**, Department of Mechanical Engineering, Virginia Tech

Ranhee Yoon presented her research on an autonomous health inspection system for power line conductors using a mobile robot. Her work integrates vision-based inspection with machine learning techniques to identify and classify different levels of surface damage on power line conductors, supporting more efficient and reliable condition-based maintenance of power infrastructure.

- [Dr. Chen](/members/jie-chen.html) received the ASME/Boeing Structures and Materials Best Paper Award for the paper “Jie Chen, Yongming Liu. Probabilistic Physics-Guided Machine Learning with Missing Data: Applications in Additive Manufacturing. 2025 ASME SSDM Conference.”

- Congratulations to [Yisheng Lu](/members/yisheng-lu.html) on receiving the Mary Virginia Jones Mechanical Engineering Graduate Fellowship.

- Congratulations to [Jun Li](/members/jun-li.html) on receiving conference funding to attend ASME SSDM from Virginia Tech Mechanical Engineering.

We developed a method for design of high-dimensional materials. We developed physics-based uncertainty-aware neural network in Bayesian Optimization. This method is used to design kirigami metamaterials to achieve zero poisson ratio and comformable wearables.

- Jinyang Li, Jyotshna Bali, Ko-Lin Wang, Suyi Li, Jie Chen, [High-Dimensional Design of Kirigami Patches Metamaterials Using Physics-based Machine Learning with Uncertainty Quantification](https://authors.elsevier.com/a/1n5jX5FA1kHGRC).
Advanced Engineering Informatics, Volume 74, Part C, 2026, 104776, ISSN 1474-0346, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aei.2026.104776.

- Jyotshna Bali, Jinyang Li, Jie Chen, and Suyi Li, [Rapid Design and Fabrication of Body Conformable Surfaces with Kirigami Cutting and Machine Learning](https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.202522787). Advanced Science, no. 16 (2026): e22787. https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.202522787


Jie Chen, Timothy Long, Michael Wall, Todd Hufnagel, and Wei Chen. [Uncertainty aware machine learning for bridging simulation and experiment in high throughput materials characterization](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-51212-8). Scientific Reports (2026).
